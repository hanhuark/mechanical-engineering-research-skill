## Repository Description

> Open, cross-agent workflow skills for thermal-fluid mechanical engineering research. The suite supports physics-aware analysis, literature synthesis, technical writing, proposal development, data analysis, scientific schematics, mentoring feedback, and iterative manuscript revision.

## Short Public Post

I maintain an open **Mechanical Engineering Research Skill** for Codex and Claude Code. It is designed for a problem that many research agents handle unevenly: connecting AI-assisted work back to physical assumptions, source quality, validation, uncertainty, and clear technical conclusions.

The repository includes focused skills for thermal-fluid analysis, literature and technical writing, proposals, data analysis, scientific schematics, student-facing feedback, and manuscript revision. It is open under the MIT License and includes public-safe examples and installation guidance.

Repository: https://github.com/hanhuark/mechanical-engineering-research-skill

## Researcher Or Lab Message

> I am sharing an open workflow-skill suite for thermal-fluid and mechanical-engineering research with AI agents. It is intended for bounded, independently checkable tasks such as literature synthesis, a figure discussion, a baseline-first DOE plan, or a CFD validity review. The repository is open and includes a quickstart: https://github.com/hanhuark/mechanical-engineering-research-skill

## Pilot Feedback Request

> If you try one task, I would value a brief note on the task type, what the workflow improved, what failed or felt unclear, and whether you would use it again. Please do not send unpublished manuscripts, confidential proposals, restricted data, or identifying student material.
