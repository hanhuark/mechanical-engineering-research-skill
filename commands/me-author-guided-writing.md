# Author-Guided Technical Writing

Use `mechanical-engineering-research` with `author-guided-ai-writing.md` for a manuscript, proposal, report, review, or response that will be drafted or revised with AI assistance.

1. Ask for or construct a one-page author story brief: reader and decision, technical problem, specific gap, reason the gap persists, present response, main evidence, claim ceiling, and reader takeaway.
2. Convert the brief into a narrative and claim-evidence map. Do not start full prose until the author approves the scientific story or records unresolved decisions.
3. Draft only from the approved evidence and sources. Preserve evidence labels, assumptions, citation support, and claim boundaries. Return unresolved evidence as questions, not invented prose.
4. Run the six reader-recovery questions in `author-guided-ai-writing.md`. Classify each as accept, revise, or evidence needed.
5. Revise in this order: evidence and validity; narrative and paragraph purpose; mechanisms, figures, equations, and literature synthesis; terminology and citations; then sentence-level clarity.

Expected output:

- author story brief and narrative map
- evidence-grounded draft or revision
- accept/revise/evidence-needed decision record
- concise list of unresolved scientific or author decisions
