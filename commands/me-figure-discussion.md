# Thermal-Fluid Figure Discussion

Use `mechanical-engineering-research` to turn a plot, image, table, or figure caption into a physically meaningful results discussion.

Workflow:

1. Identify what the figure actually shows: axes, units, cases, uncertainty, images, contours, or data-processing outputs.
2. State the main observation without overinterpreting.
3. Explain the dominant physical mechanism using the appropriate regime, dimensionless groups, transport balances, or scaling logic.
4. Compare against baseline, correlation, literature, model, or expected trend.
5. State uncertainty, limitations, and what the figure cannot prove.
6. End with the implication for design, model validity, mechanism understanding, or the next experiment.

Expected output:

- revised caption or paragraph
- claim-strength notes
- missing comparison or uncertainty checks
- figure-specific next steps
