# Thermal-Fluid CFD Review

Use `mechanical-engineering-research` to review CFD work before it is trusted as evidence in a paper, report, proposal, or design decision.

Workflow:

1. Identify governing physics: laminar, transitional, turbulent, compressible, multiphase, conjugate, reacting, radiating, or non-Newtonian.
2. Inspect geometry, boundary conditions, material properties, wall heat flux or temperature treatment, and inlet/outlet assumptions.
3. Check turbulence or multiphase models against the operating regime.
4. Check mesh independence, y+, wall treatment, residuals, conservation imbalance, and monitor convergence.
5. Compare CFD outputs against analytical limits, empirical correlations, experiments, datasheets, or benchmark cases.
6. Separate what the CFD shows from what it does not prove.

Expected output:

- CFD credibility memo
- missing setup details
- validation and sanity-check plan
- unsupported or overclaimed conclusions
- revised defensible claim wording
