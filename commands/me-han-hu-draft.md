# Han Hu Manuscript Draft

Use `mechanical-engineering-research` in Han Hu manuscript mode.

1. Identify the requested section, manuscript title, journal, audience, supplied source files, figures, data, equations, and citations.
2. Read `han-hu-research-style.md`, `paper-writing-style.md`, and `han-hu-style-calibration-protocol.md`.
3. Retrieve two to four private corpus excerpts matched to the requested genre and rhetorical function. Prefer accepted or author-approved after-text; preserve each exemplar’s evidence status.
4. Draft from the current project evidence, not from the corpus. Start with the engineering problem and mechanism before AI, software, or data infrastructure.
5. For abstracts, introductions, and conclusions, audit the narrative before sentence-level polishing: state the problem, the specific gap and consequence, what the work does, its main finding, its bounded implication, and the evidence still needed. Do not treat data integration alone or a known limitation as the contribution.
6. Preserve all supplied values, units, definitions, citations, evidence classes, and claim ceilings. Do not invent a result, mechanism, citation, validation, or approval state.
7. Return the draft, a claim-boundary note, and a style-deviation audit naming the exemplar IDs, their evidence status, any necessary journal departure, and the smallest unresolved evidence need.

Expected output:

- publication-ready draft text for the requested section
- evidence and claim-boundary note
- reader-clarity and narrative audit for abstracts, introductions, or conclusions
- style-deviation audit
