# Han Hu Manuscript Revision

Use `mechanical-engineering-research` in Han Hu manuscript mode.

1. Inspect the current manuscript source, figures and captions, tables, equations, cited evidence, reviewer comments, and available data before editing.
2. Read `han-hu-research-style.md`, `paper-writing-style.md`, `manuscript-revision-submission.md`, and `han-hu-style-calibration-protocol.md`.
3. Retrieve two to four private corpus excerpts matched to the section’s genre and rhetorical function. Prefer accepted or author-approved after-text; preserve each exemplar’s evidence status.
4. Diagnose paragraph logic, physical framing, figure roles, claim strength, definitions, conditions, evidence boundaries, and redundancy before sentence-level polishing.
5. Correct substantive problems through analysis, evidence, definitions, or explicit limitations where required. Do not merely weaken prose to hide a validity gap.
6. Preserve values, units, equations, citations, sample sizes, uncertainty statements, definitions, and evidence class. Flag any proposed change that would alter them.
7. Return the revised text, a change log separating substantive corrections from style edits, unresolved blockers, and a style-deviation audit.

Expected output:

- revised manuscript text
- substantive-versus-style change log
- unresolved technical or evidence blockers
- style-deviation audit
