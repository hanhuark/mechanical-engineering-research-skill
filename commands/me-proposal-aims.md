# Thermal-Fluid Proposal Aims

Use `mechanical-engineering-research` to sharpen proposal aims, objectives, or thrusts for thermal-fluid mechanical-engineering research.

Workflow:

1. Identify the sponsor mission, review criteria, central barrier, and proposed capability.
2. Convert vague tasks into aims with testable hypotheses or decision-driving objectives.
3. For each aim, connect methods, preliminary evidence, validation, metrics, risks, alternatives, and expected outcomes.
4. Make integration explicit when experiments, CFD, theory, AI/ML, or hardware development are combined.
5. Replace inflated adjectives with measurable advances.
6. Check whether the aims support a coherent first-page story.

Expected output:

- rewritten overall goal
- two to four aims or objectives
- aim-by-aim hypothesis, approach, metrics, risk, and output table
- reviewer-friction notes
- suggested figure or milestone plan
